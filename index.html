Network Research Engineer, ByteDance (TikTok)
Ph.D. in Computer Science, George Washington University

I am a Network Research Engineer at ByteDance (TikTok). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the George Washington University in 2026, where I worked with Prof. Timothy Wood. My research lies at the intersection of high-performance network systems and cybersecurity, with a focus on programmable data planes, high-speed traffic analysis, and scalable network security. I design and optimize systems using programmable switches and SmartNICs for high-throughput network monitoring, threat detection, and attack mitigation.
